About this study aid

Disclaimer

A personal practice companion for your Biblical Hebrew course.
Class companion, not a textbook replacement. Ivrit is intended to support study alongside your Hebrew class and assigned materials.

Course reference

This personal study tool is based on class notes and vocabulary from Beginning Biblical Hebrew: A Grammar and Illustrated Reader by John A. Cook and Robert D. Holmstedt.

Disclaimer

This website is an independent, personal project. It is not produced, sponsored, approved, or endorsed by the book’s authors, publisher, or any school or instructor. It does not replace the textbook, class instruction, or instructor guidance. When a flashcard or note differs from your course materials, follow your instructor and the assigned edition.

Some entries include traditional reading conventions or brief grammatical notes. Use the original textbook and class materials for full explanations, context, and corrections. No textbook pages or full copyrighted lesson content are reproduced here.
